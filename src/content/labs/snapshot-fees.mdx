## Why fees need rethinking

<div class="stub">

- Batch auctions: discrete windows, sealed books, no latency race
- CLOB fee logic (volume tiers) gets imported by default; why it fits badly here
- Volume tiers: incumbents get discounts, new makers pay full freight, long-tail markets crowded out
- Volume measures history, not the quality of liquidity standing right now
- A sealed window produces a complete snapshot: price the snapshot instead

</div>

## The idea: directionality is the price

<div class="stub">

- Replace maker/taker with a spectrum of directionality
- Confidently two-sided near fair value: trades free. Fully one-sided: pays the taker rate
- A one-sided resting order is a patient taker
- Everything is computed fresh from one sealed snapshot per window: memoryless, verifiable, no tiers, no identity

</div>

## The base fee: placement against a standard

<div class="stub">

- The market declares a standard: the band, M ± S/2. Placement inside it is free
- Outside the band the fee rises linearly: k per bps of imprecision, capped at F
- The cap means no resting order ever pays more than a taker
- Net edge: distance earns 1:1, the fee takes k per bps, k below 1 keeps width profitable
- Full-fee point: beyond S/2 + F/k the schedule stops caring

</div>

## One maker, two sides

<div class="stub">

- Introduce M: the mark price, measured at size by walking T dollars into each side

- Worse leg per dollar; whatever has no match behind it is directional and pays F
- Read a fee receipt: base fee, surcharges per match, total, net edge

## The Mark is communal

<div class="stub">

- In a real market M is measured from everyone's book at once
- Eligibility: only demonstrated two-sided size near the touch gets a vote
- Your voice is your share of the measuring walk
- Confident non-directionality toward the mid is a vote for M, and tighter quoting carries more weight
- Your fees come from your book, but judged against a yardstick you only partly control
- Needs its own beat: what happens when there is not enough stock to compute M

</div>

## Concerns, variations, open questions

<div class="stub">

- Semi-far coverage earns a discount: paid optionality, priced by fill risk and adverse selection
- Manipulation economics: junk coverage is worthless, bending M requires standing real paired size
- Parameters and governance: S declared and sticky, F venue-wide, k the one width dial, T measured from demand
- Variations considered: partner-stamp grace interval, asymptotic stamps (rejected: softens the junk anchor), inside compensation (parked), partial fills
- Open: cross-window dynamics, staking-based fee caps, thin-market M fallbacks
