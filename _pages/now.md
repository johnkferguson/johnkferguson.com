**When: January 16, 2016**

**Where: Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic**

Below are some of the different things I've been focusing on in my life:

**Poker:** I continue to play poker professionally. Current main game is  online 6-max No Limit Hold'em online at $3/6 & $2/5. My playing volume has been decent as of late. Recent ongoing improvements in my game have included: (1) overbetting the river more, both for value and as a bluff (2) playing more loose and aggressive from the Big Blind when facing a Button raise.

**Weightlifting:** I go to the gym 5 times per week and follow the training program outlined in [Bigger Leaner Stronger](http://www.amazon.com/Bigger-Leaner-Stronger-Building-Ultimate/dp/1938895274). I have modified the program a bit and currently do not do any squats or deadlifts as I hurt my back several months ago and am still recovering.

**Sauna:** I use the sauna a few times per week for 20 - 25 minutes after lifting weights. There are [various benefits to sauna usage](http://fourhourworkweek.com/2014/04/10/saunas-hyperthermic-conditioning-2/). For me, I've found it to be a very relaxing practice. On days when I use the sauna, I have a much easier time falling asleep.

**Intermittent Fasting:** I only eat during an 8 hour window everyday and then fast the remaining 16 hours. This is typically referred to as a 16/8 intermittent fasting protocol. In my case, I usually have my first meal at 3pm and my last around 11.

**Personal Chef/Cook:** I'm not much of a cook and I like to eat healthy, so I have a personal chef come to my place twice a week to prepare my meals and store them in the fridge. In the Dominican Republic, it's not that expensive to have someone cook for you.

**Spanish:** My Spanish is already "conversational." However, during the past few months, I've been putting in effort to try and attain fluency. The current main components of this entail:

1. Private Spanish lessons on Skype twice per week using a service called [Live Lingua](https://www.livelingua.com/).
2. Studying flashcards for Spanish vocabulary and grammar using [Anki](http://ankisrs.net/) and the methods outlined in [Fluent Forever](http://www.amazon.com/Fluent-Forever-Learn-Language-Forget/dp/0385348118).
3. General Spanish usage in daily life. The most common scenario I find myself using Spanish is talking to girls and going on dates.

I've also started reading [*El Marciano*](http://www.amazon.com/El-marciano-Spanish-Andy-Weir-ebook/dp/B00OXYQR38), which is a Spanish version of [The Martian](http://www.amazon.com/Martian-Novel-Andy-Weir-ebook/dp/B00EMXBDMA). Whenever I read it, I also listen to an [audiobook recording of it](http://www.ivoox.com/podcast-marciano-andy-weir_sq_f1148501_1.html) at the same time.

**Dating:** I've done a bit of dating during my time in Santo Domingo, mostly using online dating apps like [Tinder](https://www.gotinder.com/) and [Badoo](https://badoo.com) to meet girls. In addition, I've also met a few people through normal daily life or by going out to bars. I'm going to be putting less time into dating during the next few months as playing poker, making money, and getting healthy by going to the gym are currently much higher priorities for me.
